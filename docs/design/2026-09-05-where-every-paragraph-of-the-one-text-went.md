**The four stances:** **kept** — the paragraph stands, or stands with a word
changed; **tightened** — the same intent in fewer words, in the same place;
**moved** — the intent now lives in the section the row names; **dropped** — the
words are gone and the row says which sentence of the rewrite carries the intent,
or that it was argument the shape now does.

what makes it hard, the way, the dated failure) in every section; the four
verification and search rules in §8 with the sentence on estimates (contested,
row 43a of the Hello Revenue table); the carrier rule at the head; the trace
rules in §5; the "names the failure" clause in §4; the checking clause in §3; the
plan's "words that asked for it" (contested, row 43c); the class on a finding in

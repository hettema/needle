4 · The calls this comp makes

The executing session's — stated, made, open to veto:

  1. The strip takes the meaning's colour on its border and its first sentence, and a chosen focus is quiet. A proposal is his move (amber), a conversation is live (teal), a paused order is broken (red). Acceptance earns no green: proven is the board's word for a loop it closed itself, and a focus is a diagnosis he chose to use.
  2. The two sentences stay on the strip under every lens; the evidence, measures and history expand in place behind "more", never on a second page.
  3. The card's class is one plain phrase in monospace, with its likelihood and its hold beside it, and the reading's sentence under it — no colour, since a class is a fact about the focus and not a claim on him. "Being read" is the one live state and takes teal.
  4. A card that would move is drawn with a dashed border and says where from. The dashed border is the page's one mark for "not stored yet"; a drop under this lens is not a rank, exactly as under Age and Gate.
  5. The accept bar lists every proposed move as a tick, all ticked at first, and the one button carries the count of ticks. "Put it back" lives on the strip, since it is offered under every lens until he moves a card by hand.
  6. The Focus door sits on the strip and not in the head, beside the Idea door's twin sentence "What needs to change?", because the strip is where its answer lands.

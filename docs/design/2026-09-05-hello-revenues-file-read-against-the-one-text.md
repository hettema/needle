## How to read it

A paragraph is a blank-line block, headings and rule lines included, the same
count card #54's table used. A block whose sentences take different stances
splits into `7a`, `7b`, … with no gaps. **The six stances**, from the suggestion
this card carries:

| stance | means | what happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **the project's own** | domain truth or a specialised method of Hello Revenue's | stays, untouched by any card |
| **global wins** | the one text carries the same intent at equal or greater strength, both sentences quoted | the local copy is a candidate for the later trimming card; nothing is edited here |
| **missing portable doctrine** | a rule every project needs, supported by an observed failure the row cites | lands in the rewritten one text on this card, in the section the row names |
| **portable intent, project mechanism** | the intent travels; the mechanism stays with the project | the intent lands in the one text; the mechanism is never named there |
| **unproved claim** | a method or a causal story its evidence does not establish | surfaced here and never promoted, however explanatory it sounds |
| **contested** | it cuts against the one text, or the reader cannot say which is stronger | both readings go to the owner, neither selected |

**What is quoted and what is not.** This repository is public. A row on a
doctrine sentence quotes it, because the comparison needs both texts side by
side. A row on a product paragraph — the mission, the architecture, the
intelligence assets, the operational contracts — names it by heading and line
and quotes nothing: the stance "the project's own" is the whole ruling, and
Hello Revenue's internals are Hello Revenue's.

**Nothing in Hello Revenue's file is edited on this card.** The plan's
*Deliberately not* says so; the one defect the read found in that file — its
head points at the former global file's sections, a base that no longer exists
— is filed in that corpus as a suggestion marked a defect, fixed on its own card
after the one text lands.

**The counterexample stands.** Hello Revenue's file practises a loop once
(row 120, the suite-once ruling with its baseline, its signal and its closing
card) and never states the general obligation. The one text's §7 is stronger,
and nothing in that section moves. What Hello Revenue's file *has* that the one
text lacked is the verification discipline (rows 7, 8, 9, 10, A) and the shape
of a rule: an intent, what makes it hard, what holds it, the incident that
shaped it (rows 29–32, 41b).

**The provenance rule this read worked under:** the file is roughly twelve
months of close work and about five of them are in its git history, so the
history is not a signal for what is hard-earned. Every rule was judged on its
words and the failure recorded behind them, never on its age.

# The carriers — the hook, the output style, the readout

The doctrine that lives in a script is doctrine too. Each rule of
`.claude/hooks/backbrief.sh` is a row; the output style is five; the effort
readout is one.

#### A · missing portable doctrine
> "1. VERIFY BEFORE STATING. Ground truth = actual docs, a working example, or
> diagnostic output you just ran. A subagent's summary, a confident
> training-data recall, or your own in-session memory of code state from earlier
> turns is NOT ground truth. Neither is a docs/wiki page … Mark the epistemic
> status of every load-bearing claim: verified-just-now / recalled-from-earlier
> / inferred-from-context. Don't phrase recall or inference the same way you
> phrase verified — 'Probably / likely / I think' is a flag to stop and verify,
> not a hedge to ship under."

**Restates:** row 7b's rule with three clauses the file itself lacks: the
session's own memory of earlier turns is not ground truth; a load-bearing claim
says how it was known; a hedge is the trigger to verify, not a way to ship. §8
has *"Don't settle for 'probably'"* — a preference where this is a trigger.
**The failure:** the hook's own history
(`docs/patterns/backbrief-resonance.md:272–280`) and Codex's *"expected to work
from"* on 2026-09-05, which is exactly a hedge shipped as a fact.
**Lands in:** §8. The wiki clause is the project's.

#### B · portable intent, project mechanism
> "2. BEFORE NAMING A NEW PRIMITIVE (event, column, field, component, type),
> search for an existing one that carries the same signal … Start the search at
> docs/wiki (index.md → the seam's concept page + the generation-topology map) —
> the wiki shortens the search; the grep is still the proof."

**The intent:** row 8's. **The mechanism:** the wiki route.

#### C · global wins
> "3. SHAPE FIRST, MECHANISM ON DEMAND. Dennis is the product owner, not the
> implementer. Lead with the shape of the change … The mechanism — library
> names, dispatch patterns, slot semantics, file paths, framework internals — is
> background, not headline."

**Restates:** the owner's steering, *"Dennis directs on intent and outcome, not
mechanism. Say what changed and what it means for him — not how it works,
unless he asks."*
**Why:** the same instruction, and correctly not doctrine: another owner would
want otherwise.

#### D · global wins
> "4. LABOR ECONOMICS ARE INVERTED AND YOUR TRAINING HIDES IT. Humans take
> shortcuts … You inherit those patterns from training data but face neither
> constraint — your cost to do it right is a rounding error, and nothing stops
> you from creating drift … A bar a human would find exhausting is the low bar
> for you."

**Restates:** §3, whole. One clause is sharper and is the next row.

> "This applies to verification specifically: re-reading files you just wrote,
> re-running commands you just ran, double-checking what you 'remember' — these

**Restates:** nothing. §3 states the economics and §8 the checking; nothing

**The failure:** the hook's rule-4 comment, written after the 2026-04-20 misses.
**Lands in:** §3, one sentence.

#### F · missing portable doctrine
The output style, `general-brief.md`, read as five paragraphs of doctrine about
briefing the person:

- *"Every brief leaves him able to do one of three things with confidence:
  rule, delegate … or ask a specific question. The one outcome a brief must
  never produce is the fourth state … he cannot tell what is being asked."* —
  the owner's, from 2026-08-23 (`backbrief-resonance.md:284`). **Lands in** the
  steering section.
- The fork's four parts — *the question as a question he can answer cold; what
  is true today; what turns on it; your recommendation and its why* — his, same
  date. **Lands in** the steering section.
- *"Every claim about what exists, what changed, or what will happen stands on
  something you actually did … For any claim about what does or does not
  already exist in the system, the proof ships with the claim — what you
  searched, what you found, what you rejected."* — doctrine, rows 7b and 10b;
  **lands in** §8.
- The floors — *plain words; house shorthand is a technical name; direct and
  unhedged, hedging means you have not verified; every sentence earns its
  place; correcting yourself takes one sentence* — his; the unhedged clause is
  doctrine (row A). **Lands in** the steering section.
- *"Reason from that contract; do not restate it here, because a second copy
  would drift from the first."* and the pattern's rule, *"a restatement sitting
  in the highest-salience surface would win every conflict with the canon
  silently"* (`backbrief-resonance.md:139`; the 2026-08-07 incident, `:282`, a
  style that inverted the canon's proof-of-search into permission to omit it) —
  a rule about every carrier of the doctrine. **Lands in** the head of the
  rewrite, and it is the rule the re-anchor hook on this card is built to.

**The decision on the style as a carrier, with its alternative:** its portable
content moves into the one text, where every make reads it. Rejected: shipping
the style file itself from Needle into each project. It is Claude-only, it is
selected by a gitignored file no installer writes (so it is not reliably active
even in Hello Revenue today), it reaches no subagent, and it is the surface on
which a paraphrase once inverted the canon for months. A carrier that holds
none of the doctrine's own words has nothing to invert.

The open card
The top of the card answers the three questions in order: what is this, what is happening to it right now, and what can I do about it. The state sentence sits under the title in the state's colour. The doors row has one primary door, filled, and the rest outlined; doors that this state closes are not hidden and not buttons — they are one grey line saying when they open.
The record reads as history, newest first, with who did it: you, a session, or the machine. Carried suggestions are chips, so a folded card is one glance, not a scroll. The plan is one line with its path and a way in.
The border is teal because the card is live. On a card that is asking you it is amber, on a doubted one red, and on everything else it is the quiet line.

The colour language
Five colours, five meanings, nothing decorative.
A colour appears only where its meaning holds. A pill, a card state, a door or a rail item carries colour because the board is saying one of these five things — never because a counter wanted to look important.
Your move
Only you can act. A question from a lane, a verdict to accept, a signal only you can read, a card in Decision moment.
amber · attn
Broken
Evidence is gone or two things disagree. A doubted status, a document that is nowhere, a lane that died, two running lanes in one file. Not a collision before Start — that card simply waits.
red · wrong
Live
Happening right now. A lane with hands on, a conversation in progress, a fold landing. Never a button.
teal · accent
Proven
The loop closed. A signal read as delivered, a card in Done, a fold that landed, the trunk level.
green · landed
Quiet
Information with no claim on you. Counts, ages, gates, kinds, what arrived today, what is unplanned.
grey · ink-2/3
Where colour lives
Attention railone line, three words that are filters of the board — Your move, Broken, Live — each with its count; the breakdown appears only when one is clicked.
Card stateone word, one colour, always in the same place on the card.
Doorsnever coloured: the primary door is the filled ink button, the rest are outlined, a closed door is grey text with its reason.
The loopa shipped card carries its loop as a glyph: an open ring in ink with who reads it and when; a filled green dot with the time it was read.
Columnsnever coloured. The column is a stage, not a state.
What colour never means
A count is not amber because it is large. 102 unplanned ideas is quiet.
A category is not a colour. Defects and ideas are two rails, not two hues.
A button is not a colour. Pressable is a shape — filled or outlined — never a hue, so nothing you can press is ever confused with something that is happening.
An outline ring with a number and no word is not a signal. The head is one line and keeps its words: Your move 215 Broken 18 Live 1.

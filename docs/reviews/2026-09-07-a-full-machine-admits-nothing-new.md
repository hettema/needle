## What the build learned the plan got wrong

- The plan believed the adopt "the launch already makes" would put a killed lane back: it could not, ever — a scope oomd kills stays loaded as `failed`, and the user manager refuses a new transient unit under that name. Every resumed lane since 2026-09-05 was un-adoptable for that reason, not for want of a caller. The reset is now inside the one adopt, so the Start's re-adopt of a killed daemon gains it too.
- The plan's loop was written as a command that exits 1 on success. A loop whose signal cannot read delivered is a hope with a number attached (`HOW-WE-WORK.md` §7); the close is where that showed, and the card's WATCH row carries the corrected command.
- "Which lane is growing" needed the lane's scope name, not the session's cgroup: reading by the name the lane was given at Start is what makes the reading deterministic on the fixture and honest live (a session outside its scope contributes nothing to its lane's number until item 2 puts it back).

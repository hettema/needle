## Dispositions

1. **Item 6 as specified — `PRAGMA data_version` once a second — would have looped.** The mechanism it replaces already did: the store-file watcher heard the lane loop's own lane records land, reconciled, wrote again, and bumped the page's version 2.3 times a second on the served board (versions 1347→1405 in 25 s idle, 17:09). `data_version` cannot tell the server's pooled connections from another process either. FIXED: the write stamp (plan ruling 10); the file watcher removed; measured flat on the throwaway board and one bump for one foreign write.
2. **The archived rule, placed in `Live.rescan` as "the corpus watcher acting on a document event", would have moved a card to Decision moment a moment before `needle close` moved it to Executed** — the close rescans first, in a process that has never read the machine. FIXED: the rule lives in the lane loop with the other machine moves and never acts while a lane has hands on (ruling 1).
3. **`needle verdicts`'s test expected #259 doubted in Executing; the new rule had already moved it** (its plan archived, its 0.1-era WATCH unreadable) to Decision moment. FIXED: the test asserts the new truth on #223, which stays.
4. **The first scroll check compared the still column's card to the viewport and failed when the head folded** — every column rises when the head shrinks. FIXED: it compares the card's distance from its own heading before and after.
5. **A missing write-stamp row would have failed every write** (`scalar_one`). FIXED: the stamp remakes the row.
6. **The signal loop read every card, folded ones included.** FIXED: a folded card's loop is its leader's; skipped.
7. **A drag against the `Kind:` line would have been undone at the next sweep, in silence and at an unpredictable moment.** FIXED: `Live.move` refuses it at once, naming the line and the file (ruling 3; `test_live.py::test_a_hand_move_against_the_documents_kind_is_refused_…`).
8. **The Plan door's test read the brief through the window's shell quoting, where an apostrophe is escaped.** FIXED: the test checks phrases without one; the brief is unchanged.
9. **Two processes sweeping the same document can take the same card number** (the served board's journal, 16:29:13: `UNIQUE constraint failed: cards.project_slug, cards.number` in `apply_effects`; pysqlite begins the transaction at the first write, after the reads that chose the number). OUTSIDE THE CHANGE: filed as `docs/slice-suggestions/2026-09-04-two-processes-can-birth-the-same-card-number.md`, `Kind: defect`, with the mechanism and the fix shape.
10. **The pill carried `role="status"` and collided with the lane band's role in the page's own queries.** FIXED: a data attribute; the pill is a word with a title, not a live region.

## What the build learned the plan got wrong

- Item 6 named a mechanism (`data_version`) that could not do what its intent asked; the intent held and the method changed (ruling 10). The defect behind it was worse than the suggestion said: not a missed write but a server re-reading itself twice a second.
- Item 5 asked the watcher to archive the suggestion; the board reads the repository and never writes into it (ruling 6). The session that writes the plan does the archiving, and the board tolerates one it forgot.
- "The corpus watcher acting on a document event" was one mechanism in the plan's words and two in the build: the relink and the fold need only the corpus and run in the sweep; the archived rule needs the lane and runs in the lane loop (ruling 1).
- The suggestion's "checkbox appearing on the first selection" needed a first gesture it did not name: a `+` beside Plan on each suggestion card.

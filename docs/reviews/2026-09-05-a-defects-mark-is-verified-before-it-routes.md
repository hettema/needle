## What the build learned the plan got wrong

- **The plan said the default lives in `docs/HOW-WE-WORK.md` §8.** The sentence it names is in §10, and the ownership rule it wanted written belongs in §1 — §8 is *Verify, don't assume*. Both sections moved on this card and §8 was left alone.
- **The plan did not say what happens to the rest of the rail.** Its item 3 reads as though the seat verifies the marked defects. It verifies every defect on the rail, because an unmarked one is nobody's until something has looked at it — which is item 1's own rule. The consequence is one reading per rail defect the first time the dial runs on a project, bounded by the ceiling and one per beat, and every existing dial test had to be rewritten around it.
- **The plan asked the reading to carry the decision identity forward through the split.** It does, but the machine has to write a record of its own on the half it extracts to hold the parentage, and that record is bookkeeping rather than a proposal — a distinction the plan does not draw, and one the code has to make, or a split lane opens on the half it just created.

## Not done, stated

- **The reason bar is held from 2026-09-06, not retroactively.** Sixteen live marks in this corpus predate it. Inventing reasons for other sessions' findings would be a worse lie than an absent one, and the seat is what reads those. A second test names the grandfathered set so it cannot quietly become a resting place.
- **"`needle fixes` shows the card as `now` on the next read" (item 5's done means) is met by the card and not by that command.** After his ruling lands in the corpus the document says `now` and the card says so; `needle fixes` shows the routing, which is `stale` until a fresh reading verifies the text he changed. That is the rule working, not an omission — but it is not the sentence the plan wrote.
- **A ruling recorded against text that changes before the lane runs is never applied**, and the card says only that it is nobody's until a fresh reading. Deliberate: applying his answer to text he did not read would be worse. The fresh reading is the right next act, and the row stands in the history either way.

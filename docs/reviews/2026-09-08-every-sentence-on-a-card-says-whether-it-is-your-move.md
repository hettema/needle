## What the build learned the plan got wrong

- The plan named `api/loops.py` as a sentence module; it builds history rows and machine readings, and nothing there reaches a face. The third module was `board/collision.py`, whose sentence every face wraps as its reason.
- The plan counted sixty-three state sentences; there are forty `_state` calls and thirty-eight door builders.
- The plan's example "Nothing for you yet. This starts by itself …" put the opening's stop after "yet"; one shape for five meanings needed one punctuation, so every opening ends in a colon and the example reads "Nothing for you: this starts by itself once #20 ships. Move #20 up to have it sooner."
- The plan asked for a live check of #74's hold sentence; #74 shipped on 2026-09-07 and has no hold, so the last pass reads whatever the served board holds.

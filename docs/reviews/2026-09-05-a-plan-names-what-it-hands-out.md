FIXED: the sentence counts only at the head of a line or at a sentence boundary (after `. `, `**`, or a bold close and a space), never inside one; a test carries the plan's own words. `machine burn` still matches anywhere; on a well-formed plan the two agree, and on a malformed one the board's line on the card is what gets the plan fixed.

## What the build learned the plan got wrong

- "A verdict line on the card, in the same voice as a plan with no gate" is not a `VERDICT` row: that row is the owner's proposal on the card's fate (plan 05), and a wrong role is the board's own line. The card carries it as `Handouts.verdict`, a sentence in the board's voice on the open card and in the brief; nothing moves.
- "Read at parse time" for the roles cannot be literal: `board/` is pure and reads no file. The runtime reads the roles file, the loop carries the names in `MachineState`, and the judgment is made at assembly — which is also what lets the board say nothing before its first read (finding 5).

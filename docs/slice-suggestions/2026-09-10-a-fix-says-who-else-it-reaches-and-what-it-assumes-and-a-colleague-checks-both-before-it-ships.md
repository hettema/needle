# A fix says who else it reaches and what it assumes, and a colleague checks both before it ships

**Kind:** idea
**Fix:** his — it edits §13 of the one text (a fix line carries its reach and its premise; the reader's fourth question is the premise) and sets a loop's cadence (read at the fifth carded close on Hello Revenue after it ships); the owner ruled to try it in conversation on 2026-09-10 ("I want to try this way of working … have it as a card so we can close the loop after developing say 5 features … this is something we need to nail before we continue developing other things"), and the words of the edit are his to rule at Start
**Found by:** the owner and the closing session of Hello Revenue card #456, 2026-09-10, asking why a build of under two hours took thirty-six hours of review

## The intent it breaks

A review ends when the work holds, and the fixes a review makes are held to the same bar as the build. Today a fix is scoped by the finding a reader showed and rests on what its writer assumed about the rest of the system, and the only thing that catches a fix that missed its sibling, broke another reader of the same surface, or stood on a wrong assumption is the next full pass, an hour later. On card #456 that turned a two-hour build into thirty-six hours of review, four subscription cut-offs and six sessions, and the same pattern runs through the other records since 2026-09-05. The rule that would have stopped it is already written in §13 — a repair names its dependencies, and a cold reader asks who else calls this and what the sibling case is — and it is held by nothing: the register says it is traced by the record's fix lines, and the fix lines do not carry it.

## What done looks like

Three parts, and no cap on passes: the loop still ends by §13's rule of 2026-09-09, at whatever count the work earns.

1. **The fix line carries its reach and its premise.** A FIXED line in a review record, from a date, says who else the fix reaches (the sibling cases it covers, the other readers of the surface it changed) and what it assumes about who else writes or reads the thing and in what order — in free words, beside what was done, the way every finding line already opens with its class. A record whose fix lines lack it is refused where the record is read: the close (`api/doors.py::close`, which already refuses a code lane without its record), so it holds every project's records, not only Needle's own. The writer can still fill the line without looking; part 2 is what makes that visible.
2. **The reader falsifies the lines, not the diff.** The completeness read of a round is handed the round's FIXED lines and has one job per line: re-run the search behind the reach, name a sibling or a reader the line left out, drive the premise. Its verdict names the line that failed, and the record quotes it. The brief lives once — in the text every lane session opens with (`board/brief.py`, beside the filing rule it already carries in one sentence) — never in a session's own prose. §13's reader gets its fourth question: what does the fix assume, and what drove it.
3. **The trace the loop reads exists before the count.** A finding whose cause is a previous round's repair says so on its line, in the form `docs/reviews/README.md` sets, so the measure below is read from records by a session or a machine and never from memory (§7; card #60's precedent for the class brackets).

## The loop

*We think* a fix that states its reach and its premise, checked by a colleague of the other kind who falsifies both before the round ships, *will change* the number of passes a review spends on its own previous repairs, from thirteen of thirty-four on #456 to at most one per review, *because* twelve of those thirteen were findable at fix time by a search or a driven path and the fix's own tests cannot catch a premise their author holds. Passes per review then fall to what the work earns.

*A, fixed now:* on the five carded closes on Hello Revenue that follow this card's shipping, the closing session counts from each record (i) passes, (ii) findings whose line says a previous round's repair caused them, (iii) lines the completeness reader falsified per round. The baseline is #456 (34 passes; 13 repair-chasing) and the eight records above.

*If we see B:* more than one repair-caused finding per review, on average across the five, with the reader falsifying little — the writer is filling the lines without the act, and the representation is wrong: the door reads the lines against the diff (every file the diff touches that the reach line does not name), or the reader's verdict becomes the gate. The reader falsifying nothing while repair-caused findings continue — the reader is reading, not driving; its brief changes to require the driven path. Repair-caused findings at or under one per review — the rule stays and this card's loop closes.

*Cadence:* the fifth carded close after this ships, brought back by that closing session, unasked; a WATCH row on the card in the grammar of `board/signals.py`, written by the plan.

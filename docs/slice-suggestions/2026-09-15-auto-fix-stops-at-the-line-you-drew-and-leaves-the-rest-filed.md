## The intent it breaks

Auto-fix is your standing ruling about what enters execution without you, and today it has two settings — on or off, and how many at once — so "on" means every verified defect on the column, gravest first, until the column is empty. On Hello Revenue that is 30 cards that reach a client or money and then 48 that reach only you or a session: records, tests, checks, a retry a session pays. You ruled today that the second kind waits for a signal — a recurring production signature, a QA finding, a second sighting — and is fixed only when a session is in that code anyway. Nothing on the board can hold that ruling: the moment the top band empties, the beat walks below the line, and the only way to stop it is to remember to turn the switch off, which is a wish. What you lose while it does is the endless loop you named — lanes fixing what costs a session a retry while the column refills from below at the rate lanes read code — and the machine's four fix lanes spent on work no client will feel.

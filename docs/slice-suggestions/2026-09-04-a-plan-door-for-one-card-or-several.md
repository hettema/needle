# A Plan door, for one card or several

**Found by:** the owner, 2026-09-04: "cards that live without a plan cannot
be executed. I think we should have a 'create plan' button on the card (also
on the closed card)", and "do all the cards you created need to be separate
cards or can some be folded? That feels like an efficiency gain."
**Kind:** idea

## Observation

A suggestion is an idea written down; only a plan can be started (it carries
the gate and the "done means"). Today the step between them is a session the
owner opens by hand. And the unit of execution is the plan, not the card:
several suggestions are often one slice's worth of work, as 0.1 folded three
board findings into one plan on 2026-09-03 and as six suggestions sit on
Needle's Backlog today that are two slices' worth.

## Done means

- **Plan** is a door on every suggestion card, collapsed and open faces alike.
  It opens a conversation in the project on the slot and model the rule
  names, with the brief: write the plan for this suggestion in the project's
  plan shape (its plan-writing skill where one exists, else the shape
  `docs/plans/README.md` describes), citing the suggestion's path, with an
  effort gate; ask the owner in the window where the intent is unclear; write
  nothing else.
- **Plan these together:** on the board, selecting several suggestion cards
  (a checkbox on the collapsed face, appearing on the first selection) offers
  one Plan door for all of them; the brief lists every suggestion and says
  the plan carries them all.
- **The card follows the plan.** When a plan lands that cites a live
  suggestion, the corpus watcher relinks that suggestion's card to the plan
  — same card number, same history, the document now the plan, the
  suggestion archived beside it with "carried by <plan>" — and the card moves
  to Planned by the machine with that reason. A plan that cites several
  suggestions relinks the first and marks the others **folded into #N**: they
  leave Backlog for a folded group under the plan's card and close when the
  plan closes. Nothing is retyped; nothing needs a second card.
- The attention rail counts suggestions without a plan, so the owner sees
  the size of the unplanned pile.

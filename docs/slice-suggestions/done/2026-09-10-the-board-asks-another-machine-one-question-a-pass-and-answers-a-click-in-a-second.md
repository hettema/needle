# The board asks another machine one question a pass, and answers a click in a second
**Carried by:** docs/plans/2026-09-10-the-board-asks-another-machine-one-question-a-pass-and-answers-a-click-in-a-second.md

**Kind:** defect
**Fix:** now — the intent is written (card #83's plan: "the board feels like it is on your laptop", and its acceptance: the owner starts a card, gets its notification, opens its window without doing anything he did not do before); the fix stays inside `api/loops.py`, `runtime/service.py` and `runtime/remote.py` and removes the class — one typed observation per machine per pass, collected outside the loop's lock and applied under it in a moment, and doors that answer "opening, awaiting proof" at once and prove the one thing they touched — rather than another deadline raised.

## The intent it breaks

With the board on one machine and lanes on another, a reconcile pass asks the other machine one question per lane — `tip`, `edits`, `lane-docs`, `dispatches`, `transcript-size` — each a fresh `ssh … uv run needle <verb> --json` costing about a second, under the loop's one lock (`api/loops.py::Loops._lock`, held through `reconcile`), which every card door takes (`api/app.py`). With the board on the rented machine and a hundred lanes on the laptop a pass took a minute per project (Hello Revenue 55 s, Needle 58 s, measured at 18:1xZ) and the owner's Watch door on a card running on the rented machine answered nothing for 120 s. The sweep asks `sessions` again (`api/loops.py`, `_sweep_scopes`), recovery can repeat it, `Runtime.session(ref)` scans every machine even when a door knows its session, and Watch runs a full reconcile before answering (`api/doors.py`). Raising a deadline cannot remove a cost that is the representation's.

## What fixes it (Codex's reading, adopted as the shape)

- One typed observation per machine per pass: that machine's own `needle` answers one batch for the projects asked — lean sessions, worktrees with their tips, the active lanes' edits and documents, transcript facts, scopes, boots, room, account readings — invoking its readers in one process, never a fresh `uv run` per constituent. The answer names the machine, its boot, the request, the collection times, and which sections succeeded; a missing answer, an empty one and retained old evidence are three different values; one lane's failed read does not erase the machine's observation.
- Collection runs outside the lock, per machine, so a stalled machine delays no other; the board keeps the latest accepted observation warm and persists enough to survive a restart, showing its age. Under the lock: validate that the machine, project and card still exist and no command superseded the observation, apply, publish. An old observation never undoes a newer Stop, resurrects a forgotten machine or manufactures a sighting.
- Nothing that waits on another process runs under the door lock: ssh, registry or transcript walks, git fetch and levelling (the board's own `level_trunks` holds it around a 90 s fetch today), account probes, window proofs, notifications, launches, stops, recovery.
- A door records the request, acts on the one session it already knows outside the lock, records its proof; per-card serialisation covers the dial, recovery and the command-line writers; a slow launch answers "opening, awaiting confirmation" within a second and the proved result after.
- Keeps both rulings: another machine is asked through its own `needle`, and the board's store is the one record. Reuses `LaneSnapshot`, `_rooms`, `_last_rows`, the word lock, the clone collection outside the lock and the recovery record; no second reconciliation.

## What would settle it

Time four things apart — collection, lock occupancy, first door answer, completed effect — with the day's lane counts (75 and 30); stall the laptop for 45 s and require a door on an unrelated rented lane to answer promptly while the laptop's evidence shows as old.

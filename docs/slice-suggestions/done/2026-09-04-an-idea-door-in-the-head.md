# An idea door in the head: a conversation that starts from the board, about nothing yet

**Found by:** the owner, 2026-09-04: "how do I start a conversation from the
needle board? If I have a HR idea, or question etc, I'd like to start a
session from the board easily."
**Kind:** idea

## Observation

Every conversation door today hangs off a card (Discuss). An idea or a
question that is not yet a card has no door: the owner opens a terminal by
hand, in the right repository, on the right subscription. 0.1 had an idea
banner for this; it was retired with the rest.

## Done means

- The head of every project's board carries **Idea**: one click opens a
  terminal conversation in that project's checkout, on the slot and model the
  rule names, with a brief that says whose idea it is, that the corpus is the
  only way in (a plan into `docs/plans/`, a suggestion into
  `docs/slice-suggestions/`, with its `Kind:` line), and that the session
  writes nothing else to the repository. The window is proved like every
  door and tabs with the others.
- The conversation is listed on the attention rail as "in discussion" while
  it lives, and never counts as hands on any tree.
- The owner can type the first line into the door before it opens (an
  optional one-line box beside the button), so a question lands as the
  session's opening prompt; empty means the session asks.
- When the session writes a document, the card it becomes carries "born from
  a conversation on <date>" in its history.

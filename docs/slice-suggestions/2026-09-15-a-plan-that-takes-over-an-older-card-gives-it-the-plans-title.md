# A plan that takes over an older card gives it the plan's title

**Kind:** defect
**Fix:** now — the intent is written (`docs/plans/README.md`: "the first cited suggestion's card becomes the plan's card with its number and history", and the title rule: the plan's title is its card's title, what will be true when the plan is done; card #74: a cold reading judges every title on the card's face and keeps Start closed until one passes); the fix stays inside the corpus read that already retitles every other card (`board/reconcile.py`, the `Retitled` effect and its `CardOrigin.IMPORTED` guard); and it removes a class rather than an instance — every card born on the first board that any plan on any board takes over, not Hello Revenue's #197 alone.
**Found by:** the owner, from Hello Revenue's Idea door on 2026-09-15 (conversation 33980f33): "it looks like I can't hit start on either card?"

## The intent it breaks

When a plan carries a suggestion, that suggestion's card becomes the plan's card, and the card's face says what the plan will make true, so you can rank it and start it from the title alone. Today that holds for every card except the ones that came over from the first board: their title is never rewritten from their document, so when a plan takes one over the face keeps the suggestion's old title, the cold reading judges that old title against the plan's outcome and fails it, and Start stays closed with "title fails" — on a card whose plan is attached, gated and ready. What you lose: a plan you asked for sits at the top of Up next unable to start, the face tells you the wrong thing, and the writer is told to rewrite a title that no document holds.

## What done looks like

A plan that cites a suggestion whose card came from the first board retitles that card to the plan's title on the read, exactly as it does for a card born on this board, and the cold reading then judges the plan's title; ruling 1 keeps its force where it was made — an imported card whose suggestion is merely renamed, or whose plan predates the title rule, keeps the owner's title — and the guard says so beside itself. On the test board: an imported card whose suggestion a new plan cites shows the plan's title after one read and its title reading runs on the new title; an imported card whose suggestion is only renamed keeps its title.

# The rented machine has what every project on the board needs, and says what it lacks

**Kind:** defect
**Fix:** now — the intent is written (card #83's plan: "a lane on the rented machine is a lane like any other"; the owner, 2026-09-11: "rented should always have whatever any project needs"); the fix stays inside the machine's record, its drift check and the board's placement, and it removes the class — every project's needs, not one project's list.
**Found by:** the owner and the session answering him on the rented machine, 2026-09-11 morning, after the first night a Hello Revenue lane ran there without its settings, Docker or the Railway login and stood unfolded; the first instance is filed in Hello Revenue's corpus the same morning ("A Hello Revenue lane on the rented machine has what the laptop has").

## The intent it breaks

Work runs where the horsepower is and finishes there. Today the board places a card on the rented machine by room alone: it checks that the project's clone is there and level, and nothing else, so a lane can start on a machine that cannot load the project's settings, run its suite or reach what its plan touches — and the owner finds the card unfolded in the morning with its proof handed back to his click. What he loses is the night's work, and the trust that a card placed on the machine is a card that will finish.

## What fixes it

- Each project's record says what a machine must hold for its lanes — the tools, the logins, the settings files by path — in one place the machine's record reads; the laptop's record already holds the laptop's, and the rented machine's record lays the union for every project on the board.
- `machine check` on the rented machine reports what is missing per project, the way it reports a clone that is not level today; the board's machines line shows it beside the clone's word, and the placement rule refuses that machine for a project it cannot serve, with the reason on the card, rather than starting a lane that cannot fold.
- Settings files never cross through a repository: the record names where they live and that they are carried once by the owner's hand over the tailnet; a login goes through the browser-less flow the subscriptions used.

## What would settle it

A card of any project on the board, started on the rented machine, folds there without a hand from the laptop; `machine check` there is green for every project, and the day a project adds a need the check goes red and the placement rule says so on the card before a lane is started.

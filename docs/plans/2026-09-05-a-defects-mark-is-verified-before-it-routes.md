## Intent

A defect the machine can fix never waits on the owner, and a decision that is the owner's reaches him through a door he can open. Today an unmarked defect reads as his — the safe default, applied once by the session that filed it and never re-read — and a `his` card has no door at all, because `api/doors.py::answer` resumes a live lane's session and a parked suggestion has none. The rail's decision pile therefore drains at zero: eight live `his` defects, the oldest 41 days, and zero `answered` rows in the board's life. Re-read under the rule the owner ruled on 2026-09-05, five of the eight were never his. After this plan: an unmarked defect is nobody's yet and says so; a mark names a reason a ratchet can refuse; an independent reading verifies the cited source before the dial acts, bound to the exact text it read; a row can make routing stricter at once but never looser until a commit says so; a settled half never waits behind an unsettled one; a genuine `his` card opens to his sentence from the board and his answer is applied through the ordinary fold; and one command follows every colleague-taken decision to its fate with the direction it moved the product, so the loop can see drift.

The rule, in the words the owner ruled true:

> A decision is Dennis's only when the written record does not select among materially different outcomes he owns, or when acting would create external exposure beyond a bound he has already authorised. Applying an existing intent, ruling, precedent or authorised bound is execution, not a new decision. Effect-level reversibility is evidence about how safely to act under uncertainty; it is never the test of who owns the call.

Done means: an unmarked defect appears under `needs triage` in the rail and on its card, in neither the his pile nor the auto-fix queue; a test proves the CLI, the dial and the page read the same state from the one function; §8 reads the rule above.
**Met:** `domain/triage.py::Routing` is the six-state type and `board/triage.py::routing_of` the one function; the dial (`board/dial.py::why_not_eligible`), the page (`board/assemble.py::routing_for` → `CardSummary.routing` → `Kind`) and the terminal (`needle fixes`, `needle decisions`) all branch on it and nothing anywhere matches the words. ### 2. A mark names a reason the ratchet can refuse — and the ratchet is not the proof

The cheap check: a `now` or `his` whose why is empty or only a category word — "product call", "prompt change", "new surface", "UX", "bound" with nothing after it — is refused by Needle's ratchet (`tests/ratchets/test_every_suggestion_names_its_kind_and_fix.py`) with the line named; other projects learn the line from the briefs, as the ratchet's docstring already says. The lexical forms (a backticked path, a card number) are accepted affordances and are named nowhere as proof of validity: item 3 is the proof. Done means: fixtures for an empty why, a category-only why, and both valid forms; the two bad ones are refused; no reader anywhere treats a source-shaped why as a verified source.
**Met:** `board/triage.py::why_is_a_reason` refuses an empty reason, a category word and a reason too short to be one; `tests/ratchets/test_every_suggestion_names_its_kind_and_fix.py` holds Needle's corpus to it and carries the four fixtures, and a third test proves `docs/no-such-plan.md` passes the lexical reader and resolves nowhere. The bar is in `board/brief.py::FIX_BAR`, so every brief that may file a defect carries it. **Deviated:** the bar is held from 2026-09-06, not retroactively — sixteen live marks predate it, and inventing reasons for other sessions' findings would be a worse lie than an absent one; the reading seat is what reads those, and a second test names the grandfathered set so it cannot quietly become a resting place.
Hands out: execution — the ratchet suite and the parser tests after each change, every failure verbatim; verifies by re-running the one failing test it names before acting on it.

### 3. The triage seat: one independent reading, one typed result, bound to what it read

A third windowless kind, `SessionWork.TRIAGE`, with the lifecycle the other two have and the guards they lack: at most one open triage per card, tended, timed out, rescued and ended like a reading; it counts against the dial's ceiling, because it is a live session on a machine whose ceiling is memory; the dial reports "being triaged" while it runs; a triage that dies leaves the card `needs triage` with the reason on the card, retried by the machine, never inherited by the owner. The brief carries the rule, the document, and the cited source's actual text as the reading resolved it — or the fact that it could not — and asks one question: does the source select the outcome?

The result lands through one verb, `needle triage`, built beside `api/doors.py::reading`: it validates the typed result, proves an open triage exists for this card, writes a `RowKind.TRIAGED` row on the record half, ends that exact record, and reconciles, in one act. The results and what each must name: `now — <the resolved source and the proposition in it that selects the outcome>`; `his — <the alternatives, which owner-held outcome differs between them, and why no written ruling selects one — or the exact exposure and the missing authorised bound>`; `when — <trigger in the WATCH grammar>`; `split — <the two halves, each with its source>` (item 4); `cannot tell — <the missing evidence and where it should come from>`, which stays `needs triage` and routes to the owner only if the missing thing is itself his decision. A `now` on a bound names the artefact, the bound, the action's measured exposure and the comparison showing it inside. An absent or unresolvable source cannot produce `now`.

Every row carries two fingerprints: the suggestion text it classified and the source text it relied on. Eligibility rejects a row whose either fingerprint no longer matches the corpus, and the card returns to `needs triage` — a second pair of eyes verifies today's proposition, never yesterday's. A row may make routing stricter at once: `triaged his` or `cannot tell` on a document marked `now` closes the dial immediately. A row never makes routing looser than the corpus: `now` on a document marked `his` or `when` authorises nothing until a session has rewritten the mark in a commit that cites the row (item 5's path); the row records the verified decision, the document remains the routing fact. The seat is exposed as a typed runtime request that names no make; #58 decides who fills it during its experiment. Independence of context is the permanent requirement.

Done means: a `now` defect the dial plans carries a `TRIAGED now` row naming a resolved source; fixtures cover agree, stricter, looser-then-commit, `cannot tell`, a second triage refused while one is open, a triager that dies leaving `needs triage` with its reason, and a valid row followed by a document edit that the dial refuses until retriage; `needle fixes` shows a card as "being triaged" while its session runs and counts it against the ceiling.
**Met:** `SessionWork.TRIAGE` with the reading lifecycle (`api/dial.py::_triage`, `_follow_triages`, `_wants_a_reading`), a duplicate guard in the store rather than in every caller (`Store.open_windowless_session` refuses a second open record per card and kind), and `running(..., triaging=)` counting it against the dial's number. `Doors.triage` is the one atomic verb beside `Doors.reading`: it validates, fingerprints the document and the source it resolved, writes the record and the `TRIAGED` row, ends that exact reading and reconciles. `tests/api/test_the_triage_seat.py` covers agree, stricter, looser-then-commit, cannot-tell, a second reading refused, a reading that dies, a document edited under a valid row and a source that moved under one; `tests/api/test_dial.py` covers the ceiling and the card's *being triaged* face.

### 4. A split is a typed finding, applied by a lane that authorises neither half

The triage reading never edits the corpus. When a document holds an outcome the record selects beside one it does not, the result is `split`, naming the two halves and their sources. Both halves return to `needs triage`; the triage that proposed the split authorises neither. Done means: the Meta selection split of 2026-09-05 (HR `59661dcd9`, card #435) is reproduced by a fixture from a two-item document, ending with two cards both `needs triage`, and the founding case is cited in the brief.
**Met:** `TriageResult.SPLIT` opens a corpus lane in its own worktree (`Doors.corpus_lane`, named `split-<n>-<slug>` so neither the lane-directory reader nor the branch reader sees a card lane), whose brief is the row and the document and cites Hello Revenue `59661dcd9` and card #435. The board reads the corpus for whether it landed — the `Split from:` line on the extracted half — writes `SPLIT` rows on both cards with the decision identity, and leaves both halves unread: `tests/api/test_the_triage_seat.py::test_a_split_is_separated_by_a_lane_and_both_halves_come_back_unread`.

### 6. Every colleague-taken decision has one identity and one direction, readable from one command

A decision identity is minted when a `TRIAGED` row is written and carried through `SPLIT` rows, the carried plan, the fix-lane record, the fold and any later defect or revert, so a split's two halves are followed separately from the row that created them. Every `TRIAGED now` and every applied answer records the direction it moved the product, from a small fixed set: surface added or removed; strictness raised or lowered; automation increased or decreased; a spend or risk bound used; or no direction, because it restored an invariant already written. `needle fixes` lists every decision a colleague took off the owner's rail, in order, with its source, its direction and its fate. Done means: a fixture takes one triage through a split and proves one command follows both the residual and the extracted `now` card to separate fates; the first five and the first ten are readable with their directions, so the cold audit below is an act and its drift test has evidence to read.
**Met:** a decision identity is minted at each `TRIAGED` record and carried by the `SPLIT` rows, the extracted half's own record (`parent`), and the fix lane the dial opens (`fix_lanes.decision`). Every `now` records a direction from `domain/triage.py::Direction`, and `needle decisions [--first N]` lists every reading in order with its source as it resolves today, its direction, where the card routes now and its fate — read off the fix lanes, the lane records and the corpus, so no second ledger can drift from them. `tests/api/test_the_triage_seat.py::test_one_command_follows_a_split_decision_to_both_fates` takes one triage through a split and follows both fates from one identity.
Hands out: search — the audit rows, the fix lanes and the corpus documents behind each listed decision, returned as rows for the reader to join; verifies by reading one listed card's history against the row it produced.

## Acceptance criteria

1. An unmarked defect routes to nobody, reads `needs triage` from one function in the CLI, the dial and the page, and the doctrine's one text says so.
2. A `now` or `his` with an empty or category-only why is refused by the ratchet on this corpus; nothing treats a source-shaped why as verified.
3. No `now` defect enters a dial plan without a fresh `TRIAGED now` row naming a resolved source; a stricter row closes the dial at once; a looser row authorises nothing until a commit rewrites the mark; a stale row returns the card to `needs triage`.
4. A second triage cannot open on a card while one is open; a dead triager leaves `needs triage` with its reason; triage counts against the ceiling and shows on the card.
5. The owner answers a parked `his` card from the board, a lane flips the mark in a commit citing his row and folds by fast-forward, the four failures leave the row standing and retry once, and the first `answered` row on record exists.
6. `needle fixes` follows a split decision to both fates by one identity, with a direction on each; backend, ratchet, TypeScript and frontend suites green.

## Loop

We think an independent reading that verifies a mark against the text it actually read, before the dial acts, and a door on a parked `his` card whose answer is applied through the ordinary fold, will move the owner's decision pile from a stock that grows ~0.2 per fold and drains at zero to one that drains faster than it grows, because five of eight parked decisions were execution mislabelled by the session that found them, and the three that were his had no way to reach him. Fixed now, before any data: the owner audits the first five and the first ten colleague-taken decisions cold, from item 6's list, before seeing their outcomes. The classifier fails if any decision exceeded an authorised exposure bound; chose between materially different owner outcomes with no written source selecting one; was defended as reversible at the code level while its effect was not; if he would have decided differently on more than one of the first ten; or if item 6's directions show the set moving the product one way while no single citation said so. On failure the response is not revert — once a customer has seen something there is nothing to revert — it is: stop downgrades in that class, reclassify the open pile, show him the accumulated direction, tighten the rule on the observed mismatch, and resume only after a revised classifier passes a fresh predeclared sample. If after twenty decisions the pile still grows, the rule is wrong, not the owner, and this card reopens.

## Deliberately not

- A router that edits doctrine or rewrites a mark without a row a person can read, or a row that loosens routing without a commit.
- A triage that edits the corpus, chooses a document's decomposition, or approves a half it created: the reader verifies, blocks or proposes a split, and a lane does the writing.
- A fourth `Fix:` mark; an unmarked document stays meaningful and the typed state lives beside the mark.
- Two AIs on every card, or a make named in the triage seat; #58 decides whether different make earns its place, on this card's rows.
- A windowless session that writes the repository from the main checkout; the ordinary lane is the corpus-write path.
- Hello Revenue's own ratchet; HR learns the line from the briefs and changes its ratchet on its own card.
- The quiet-machine rule (#43), which gates this card's lane like every Needle lane.
- Reversibility as the ownership test; it is evidence in the triage brief, and the suggestion's Rejected section says why.

# 01 — The map

**Status:** DONE — 2026-09-03, folded into main. Review: `docs/reviews/2026-09-03-the-map.md`.
**Written:** 2026-09-03, the founding evening. Terrain: the first board (Needle 0.1) and its 8,800 lines of tests, read that day; the owner's intent in `docs/INTENT.md`.
**Effort gate:** xhigh — the hard part is deciding what is right, not doing what is specified: the first architecture of a project meant to be maintained by sessions with no memory, a design system that has to carry every later slice, and a kanban interaction that the owner will judge in product terms. Nothing here is scaffolded by existing ratchets; this slice writes the first ones.
**Sequencing:** first. Slices 02 (the runtime) and 03 (the doors and the loops) build on the types and the store this slice lands.

## Intent

The owner opens one page and sees everything on Hello Revenue's plate, ranked, in the columns he already thinks in; drags a card to change its priority or its stage; opens a card and reads what it is for without reading code. The page is true to the project's written record — its plans and suggestions — and to nothing else, because in this slice nothing runs yet.

This slice is the surface. It has to be good enough that the owner prefers it to 0.1 for reading and planning on day one, with no runtime behind it. If it is not, the sequence stops here and this plan is amended.

### 1. A project is a corpus of plans

Needle knows a project as a path to a repository that keeps plans in `docs/plans/` (live) and `docs/plans/done/` (archived), and suggestions in `docs/slice-suggestions/` with the same split. A card is born from a document; a document is the card's substance. The board stores what the document cannot: column, position, rulings, and the rows a session or the owner writes on the card.

**The corpus is the only way in (owner ruling 2026-09-03).** A plan or suggestion written by any session, anywhere, becomes a card the moment the file lands in the project's plans or suggestions folder; a plan into Planned, a suggestion into Backlog. Needle watches the corpus and never writes a plan. A card keeps its number when its document is renamed or archived, so identity follows the document, not the path.

Done means: Hello Revenue is registered as the first project by its path; every live plan and suggestion in it is a card, and a file added while the board is running is a card within seconds, without a click; a card opens to the document's title, its intent section, its effort gate and its status line, rendered from the file; when a document moves to `done/` the card knows. The 0.1 card file (`docs/board/needle-board.json` in the Hello Revenue repo) is imported once — column, position, and rows — so the owner's existing ranking is the starting state, and nothing in Needle writes to that file.

### 2. The grammar of the columns

The columns are the owner's, from 0.1: Backlog (written ideas), Planned (written plans waiting their turn), Up next (what we should do next, owner-ranked), Executing, Decision moment, Executed, Done, Not now. In this slice every move is the owner's, because nothing runs. The grammar that later slices will enforce by machine is written here as the column definitions the page shows.

Done means: the eight columns render with their definitions one hover away; a card's column and position are the board's state, persisted the moment they change, and survive a restart; a document with no card and a card with no document are both visible as such, never silently dropped.

### 3. The kanban is a real kanban

Drag and drop between and within columns, with the drop position obvious before release; keyboard reordering; sorting within a column by the owner's rank, by age, by gate; a card that shows at a glance its number, title, gate, and one line of essence, and expands in place to its intent, its core points, and its rows; every action a card offers reachable from the card without leaving the board. On a laptop screen and on a wide one.

Done means: the owner ranks a column by drag alone, without a menu; a dropped card lands where the preview said; expanding a card never scrolls the board away from it; the frontend holds one design system in `frontend/src/components/ui/` and every card, column and chip is built from it — a ratchet refuses a raw colour or a bespoke primitive outside it.

### 4. The board is true and durable

Every move is persisted before the page says it happened, and the page never shows a state the store does not hold. State lives in the board's own store, outside any project's working tree — 0.1 kept it inside the project repo and lost it twice to a reset of that tree.

Done means: a move survives a backend restart and a page reload; a write that fails is shown as failed on the page, never as success; the store carries an audit row per change (what moved, from where, to where, when, by whom — the owner or a session), and the page can show a card's history from it.

### 5. Built to be maintained by sessions with no memory

Done means: backend layered as what things are (`domain/`), what happens (`board/`, pure over typed inputs), durable execution and storage (`infrastructure/`), thin API (`api/`); frontend types mirror backend Pydantic types one module per concept; ratchets from the first commit for the boundaries in `CLAUDE.md` (no `subprocess` under `board/`, no deferral markers, no raw colours, no `any`); a `README` a session can run the project from in three commands.

## What this slice does not do

Nothing runs. No Start, no sessions, no windows, no model rule, no machine moves, no alarms. Those are slices 02 and 03 and their absence is the point: the surface is judged on its own.

## Terrain

- **0.1's grammar and its incidents:** the Hello Revenue repository, `docs/board/README.md` — the intent section at the top is this project's intent; the mechanics below it are what 0.1 did, with the incident behind each. Read for the column rulings, the card shape (`t`, `deep`, `b` rows, `gate`, `ch`), and the "three origins of a card" rule. `docs/board/needle-board.json` there is the import source for item 1.
- **0.1's tests as scenarios:** `tests/scripts/test_needle_board*.py` in that repository. For this slice the relevant families are the store (locking, idempotent row writes, gate as a field never a row), the alarms about documents without cards and cards without documents, and the page behaviours. Read them as "what a board can get wrong", not as code to port.
- **The plan document shape** the parser must read: a `#` title, a `**Status:**` line, an `**Effort gate:** <level> — <why>` line, an `## Intent` section. Suggestions share the title and a `**Found by:**` line. Both exist by the hundred in Hello Revenue; parse what is there, never demand a new format of the corpus.
- **Stack decisions, made:** Python via `uv`, FastAPI, Pydantic v2, SQLAlchemy + Alembic on SQLite for the store; React + TypeScript + Vite + Tailwind for the page; `@dnd-kit` for drag and drop (accessible, keyboard-capable, no jQuery-era baggage). Versions are the executing session's call and live in the manifests.
- **Design:** the owner judges UI in product terms. Before building the page, a signed comp: one static HTML mock of the board and one of an expanded card, shown to him, iterated, then built. 0.1 was built from a comp; the comp is where its column grammar was settled, and that step is why the surface held while the runtime did not.

## Close-out

Each item's "done means", stanced before the fold, with the evidence.

1. **A project is a corpus of plans** — met. `needle add the Hello Revenue repository` registers the project by its path, imports the 0.1 card file once (132 cards, 97 linked, 26 retired numbers, numbering from 262; the import refuses to run twice) and cards every live document (121 born at registration). While `needle serve` runs, a file written into the corpus is a card within two seconds (`tests/infrastructure/test_corpus.py`); an open card shows the document's title, every head field including the gate with its reasoning and the status line, and its intent section rendered from the file; a document moved to `done/` leaves an audit row and the card's chip reads Archived. Nothing in Needle writes to the Hello Revenue repository.
2. **The grammar of the columns** — met. Eight columns with their definitions one hover away (`domain/column.py`, shown by the page); a card's column, group and position are the store's, persisted in the same transaction as their audit row, and survive a restart (`tests/infrastructure/test_store.py`); a card citing a document that is nowhere reads "Document gone" in red and is counted on the attention line; a document with no card is listed on the page as the watcher's failure.
3. **The kanban is a real kanban** — met. Drag between and within columns with a gap that names the rank before release, verified by a driven pointer drag (`tools/drag_check.py`) and by the keyboard scenarios; keyboard lift, move, drop and cancel; Rank, Age and Gate lenses that never write; a card at rest shows number, title, gate, two lines of essence and its document state, and expands in place to 560px without the board scrolling away; the open card's actions are the three this slice can honestly do. One design system in `frontend/src/components/ui/`, held by `tests/ratchets/test_one_design_system.py`.
4. **The board is true and durable** — met. A move is persisted before the response that the page renders from; a write that fails stays on the card as "Not moved" with the store's own words and a Retry (`frontend/tests/board.test.tsx`, and `tests/ratchets/test_the_page_shows_only_held_state.py` refuses any other way of setting board state); the store lives under `~/.local/share/needle/`; every change is an audit row and the open card's History shows them.
5. **Built to be maintained by sessions with no memory** — met. `domain/`, `board/` (pure; a ratchet refuses a process), `infrastructure/`, `api/`; TypeScript types generated from the Pydantic models with a ratchet on drift; ratchets for every boundary in `CLAUDE.md` from the first code commit; a README with the three commands.

What this close leaves for the owner's read, from the review: the five working columns need 1,536px, so a 1,440px laptop scrolls sideways; the definitions of Executed, Done and Not now were written by the build session and are his to correct.

## Rulings

The design calls made on this slice, each with the alternative it rejected, so
the next session extends the surface the way this one built it. The first five
are the owner's, given at the comp sign-off on 2026-09-03 ("I'm going with your
recs across the board"); every ruling after them is the build session's,
recorded as it was made.

**1. A resting card shows two lines of essence, and there is no density
toggle.** Owner, 2026-09-03. Rejected: one line (eight cards per column instead
of six) and three lines; a toggle between them — a toggle is a second way to
read the same board, and the count that fits a screen is a decision, not a
preference.

**2. "Note" is the label for a card with nothing written behind it.** Owner,
2026-09-03. Rejected: "Unwritten", "Ask", and no label at all — the intent
says a card with no plan or suggestion behind it is a note and the board says
so, so the word on the chip is the word in the intent.

**3. Executed, Done and Not now start folded, with their counts, on a laptop;
all eight columns start open on a wide screen.** Owner, 2026-09-03. Rejected:
all eight always open with the board scrolling sideways — the five working
columns fit a laptop only if the archived cards give up their width, and the
rail keeps the count visible so nothing is hidden, only narrow.

**4. An open card widens its column to 560px and expands where it sits.**
Owner, 2026-09-03. Rejected: 720px with two neighbours pushed off-screen (less
board kept while reading one card); a modal or a side panel (loses the board,
and losing the board is losing the point).

**5. An empty section on an open card still renders, as one grey line saying
nothing is there.** Owner, 2026-09-03. Rejected: empty sections collapsing —
a section that vanishes moves every section below it, so the same fact lives
in a different place on every card and the owner re-hunts each time.

**6. A column is an ordered list of groups, one of which may be unnamed, and a
card lives in exactly one.** Build session, 2026-09-03. Rejected: groups
derived from the cards in them — an emptied group would vanish and could no
longer receive a drop, and 0.1's "a named group receives the card even when it
is empty" would be unexpressible. Groups are imported from 0.1 as they were;
this slice offers no way to create one.

**7. A card born from the corpus lands last in its column's unnamed group; a
column without one gets one at its end.** Build session, 2026-09-03. Rejected:
born cards at the top of the column (119 untriaged suggestions would sit above
the owner's ranked groups); a board-owned "Arrived" group (invents a group the
owner never made). "Move to" from the open card lands the same way.

**8. A document's identity is its stem, then its title.** Build session,
2026-09-03. A card keeps its number when its file is archived (same stem, in
`done/`) or renamed (same title, new stem, same kind). Rejected: identity by
path (an archive would orphan every card); by content hash (an edit would be a
new card). A stem that vanishes with no title match is "Document gone", and a
new title is a new card.

**9. A plan that names `**Card:** #N` becomes that card's document instead of a
new card, and the card stays where it is.** Build session, 2026-09-03. A plan
also supersedes a suggestion the card cites, because planning is what promotes
an idea. Rejected: a second card for the same work; moving the card to Planned
by machine (every move in this slice is the owner's).

**10. A card's gate is the document's when the document names one, else the
card's own.** Build session, 2026-09-03. Rejected: 0.1's repair that re-copied
the plan's gate onto the card on every read — two copies of one fact, and a
repair that wrote to the board.

**11. The 0.1 import keeps every ranked thing and drops the runtime.** Build
session, 2026-09-03. Kept: column, group, position, rows, tags, every citation
(the first plan or suggestion is the link), `nextId` as the next number, the 26
retired numbers as audit rows. Dropped: `lanes`, `lane`, `progress`, and the
three machine-born asks (#204, #205, #206) — Needle states those facts as
counts on the attention line, and an imported ask would be a note nobody ever
withdraws. Rejected: importing the asks as notes.

**12. Row kinds are a closed enum, each declaring its half.** Build session,
2026-09-03. The 23 labels in 0.1's file are the enum; a label the board has no
type for refuses the whole import with the label named. Rejected: free-text
labels (the page could not know where a row belongs).

**13. Cards born at registration are founding, not arrivals.** Build session,
2026-09-03. The NEW mark and the "arrived today" count apply only to cards the
watcher births while the board is running. Rejected: NEW on 121 cards the first
day, which would have drowned the first real arrival.

**14. The store is `$XDG_DATA_HOME/needle/needle.db`, and `add` refuses a store
inside the project it registers.** Build session, 2026-09-03. Rejected: a store
in the repository (0.1 lost it twice); a config file to point at it
(`NEEDLE_DB` is enough).

**15. The page learns of a change from one stream carrying a version number,
and refetches the board.** Build session, 2026-09-03. Rejected: polling (0.1's
trap); pushing the state over the stream (a second serialisation of one
truth). When the stream is down the header says so and dates the board it
shows.

**16. "Open the plan" renders the whole file on the card; "Copy path" copies the
absolute path and says whether it did.** Build session, 2026-09-03. Rejected: a
`file://` link (a browser blocks it from an http page, silently); an "open in
editor" door (that is the runtime's, slice 02); a disabled button.

**17. Every head field of a document renders in the plan block, in file
order.** Build session, 2026-09-03. Rejected: only the comp's three (Status,
Effort gate, Sequencing) — the corpus has Origin, North-star, Found by and
twenty more, and the rule is "the file, whole and unedited".

**18. The frontend's types are generated from the Pydantic models, one module
per concept, and a ratchet refuses drift.** Build session, 2026-09-03.
Rejected: hand-written mirrors (they drift at runtime, not at `tsc`).

**19. A column folds after eight cards behind "+ N more"; a lift into it unfolds
it.** Build session, 2026-09-03. The comp shows the row; eight is the count a
laptop shows before the column scrolls. Rejected: every card always (Backlog is
148 tall); a per-group fold (the owner's groups are short; the tail is the long
part).

**20. The lens disables dragging.** Build session, 2026-09-03. Sorted by age or
gate, a drop position is not a rank, so a card cannot be lifted until the lens
is back on Rank; the column head says which lens is on. Rejected: dragging
under a lens with the rank implied (the preview would lie).

**21. Feature components carry no class names, styles, colours or form
elements of their own.** Build session, 2026-09-03. The design system is the
comp's CSS carried over once, with Tailwind supplying the reset and a token
theme with the default palette wiped; a ratchet refuses `className`, `style`,
a raw colour, or a bare `<button>` outside `components/ui/`. Rejected:
utilities in feature components (two ways to style one thing).

**22. 0.1's light HTML in rows becomes text with two marks at import, and one
inline renderer draws it.** Build session, 2026-09-03. Rejected: storing HTML
and rendering with `innerHTML` (a second renderer and an injection surface on
a board sessions write to).

**23. The Age lens shows the oldest first; the Gate lens shows xhigh first.**
Build session, 2026-09-03. Age is the document's date from its file name, else
the day the card was born. Rejected: newest first (the board is for finding
what has waited longest).

## Intent

A lane that ended by the machine's hand — a wall, a kill, a process gone with no hook — comes back when the reason has passed, through the gate Start passes, once per cause; a lane that ended by its own work is left alone and the owner is asked; and every word the card says about how a lane ended is read at the end, from the evidence that held the process. HOW-WE-WORK §11: a move that follows a machine fact is the machine's, and a board the owner has to move by hand lies while he is away. Plan 03 wrote "the owner's choice is Resume or Look, never a guess" for a memory kill on 2026-09-04, before the dial existed; the intent in it — never a guess — is kept: the machine resumes only a death it can name.

What does not change: ruling 5 of the many-lanes plan — a dead lane's session is not stopped, its state is evidence, and the evidence is read and written on the card before any resume; the rescue horizon and the once-per-cause count; the Resume and Look doors for a lane that ended by its own work.

### 1. The reason a lane ended is read at the end, from the scope that held the process, and a fold is not a death
Done means: a session row that has never had a pid yields no death reason (the discriminator is a row that *had* a pid and now has none); the reason is computed when the session actually ends, never cached from first sight, so `the registry says: starting…` cannot outlive the birth it described; a lane scope's journal line older than the session's last life is not this death's cause, and a session that died with its daemon scope names that kill and its time (#435's second ending at 20:29Z on 2026-09-05 cited the lane scope's 18:37Z kill; the session had run in `claude-daemon-hrme.scope` since 18:38Z); a lane that folded is reported as finished — its sentence leads with the fold and names no cause of death, and the `died` line under the band is for a lane that died; a fold with no close keeps its own sentence ("the work folded, no session wrote it up"), a fold never proves a close. The death and what the turn ended with are two findings, never one: the death is named from what took the *latest* life — the scope's kill with its time, the daemon's kill, or a life that ran in a previous boot and did not return (`journalctl --list-boots`, the boot id at the session's last sighting) — and never from the registry's recorded state or a progress note, which say what the session was doing, not why it ended; an epitaph written at first sight is revised when evidence that arrives later names the death. A resumed session is told the truth: `runtime/launch.py::CONTINUE` ("the subscription ran out") only for a wall; a boot's own words for a boot, with "read your worktree before trusting your memory of it" (#85's four lanes). Tests for each.
**Met:** `runtime/reasons.py::cause_of` is the one namer, and it reads at the end: the wall detector's file by its own `why` (`domain/slot.py::Handoff.cause`, the three writers told apart — `tests/runtime/test_reasons.py::test_a_handoff_says_what_it_asks_for`), the dated journal of the lane's space and of the account's daemon space from this life's first sighting or the registry's birth of this id on (`test_a_kill_older_than_this_life_is_not_its_cause_and_the_daemons_kill_is`, #435's shape, and on this laptop `journalctl --user -u claude-daemon-hrme.scope --since` names the 17:59Z kill of 2026-09-05 that took four lanes), the boot list when a live process went down with the laptop (`test_a_process_alive_when_the_laptop_went_down_died_with_it`, #85's shape from a sighting and from a transcript alike), and otherwise *the process disappeared after its last activity at T; the cause is not established* (`test_a_wall_a_stop_and_nothing`, where the registry's `done` names nothing). The producers of *the registry says:* and *finished its turn* are gone. A row with no sighting younger than the verify window is a birth, not a death (`api/loops.py::_name_deaths`), and a death not settled is re-read on every pass until the horizon (`tests/infrastructure/test_ending_store.py::test_a_death_is_rewritten_while_unsettled`). The one widening of the discriminator: a row with no sighting that is older than the window is named from what the machine holds — the transcript's last record, the journals, the boots — because the standing pile predates the sightings, and ruling 4 reads it with this reader; a sighted row is named from its sighting. A fold leads its lane's sentence and names no death (`tests/board/test_lane.py::test_a_session_with_no_process_is_an_ended_lane_with_the_machines_reason`), and a resumed session is told the cause's own words, `CONTINUE` for a wall alone (`tests/api/test_doors.py::test_a_lane_killed_otherwise_carries_the_machines_reason_and_comes_back`, the boot case in `tests/api/test_work_the_laptop_interrupted_comes_back.py`).

### 2. What the board knows about deaths and parks lives in the store
Done means: `_deaths` and `_parked` are rows every `needle` command and `needle serve` read from the store, not sets in one process's head; the park note lands once per run-out across a restart of `needle serve` and across a `needle` command that builds its own `Loops`; a test starts a fresh `Loops` over a store already holding a park and writes nothing new (card #196 carried the note seventeen times; #409 four times tonight). `_released` and `_scoped` are the same class and go the same way, each row tied to the process life it was said for. A recovery in flight is a store row written before the launch, because `runtime/launch.py::move` launches and verifies before `_settle` records the rescue: a `needle serve` that dies between the two, or a second process, finds the replacement before making another, and one interruption has at most one live replacement; a test crashes the loop between launch and record and restarts it.

The crash itself is not thrown inside the launch on the floor — the row it leaves is what is tested.

### 5. A lane the machine ended is resumed by the machine, once per cause, through the Start gate
Done means: a named interruption of the latest life — a wall, oomd's kill of the lane scope or of the daemon scope holding it, a life that ran in a previous boot and did not return, a connection the machine recovered — on unfinished work with no current owner stop and no owner decision outstanding, is resumed by the lane loop through the runtime's resume (`runtime/service.py::resume`, the Resume door's act) with a placement with headroom and the memory floor satisfied on a fresh read; once per cause within the rescue horizon, and a second death of one cause within it parks with the reason. The count follows the lane's cause across replacement processes: a replacement's new session id is not a fresh budget; a launch that fails counts; admission refused before a launch is a wait, not an attempt; a retry whose recorded time lies in the future of the clock keeps its count and the card names the two times (today `_rescue`'s subtraction reads a future attempt as "within the hour" and says so falsely). An ending nothing names is written as "the process disappeared after <its last activity, at T>; the cause is not established" and is never resumed by the machine. The dial's "took it once already; it is the owner's from here" (`board/dial.py::why_left`) counts a lane the machine ended as not yet run, and keeps its reason for a lane that ended by its own work — a close, a question to the owner, stopped by the owner; the card's row names the cause and the count each time.
**Met:** `api/loops.py::_recover` is the one path, over a live handoff and an ended lane alike, through `Runtime.resume` (the Resume door's act) with the rung the rule answers now and the floor on a fresh read (`headroom_now`), once per cause within `RESCUE_HORIZON_SECONDS`; the count is the `recoveries` rows of the card and the cause, so a replacement's new id is no fresh budget, a launch that failed counts, and a wait before any launch is no row; a row recorded after the clock is kept and the card names both times. A second death of one cause parks on the clock until the hour clears (`test_twice_the_same_cause_within_the_hour_parks_on_the_clock_and_a_fresh_loop_writes_nothing`); an ending nothing names is written as unresolved and never resumed (`test_an_ending_nothing_names_is_shown_unresolved_and_never_resumed`); the owner's own terminal and a worker of the other make are never the board's to bring back. The dial's *took it once already* excludes a lane the machine ended (`api/dial.py::_ran`) and `_follow_lane` leaves such a lane to the board. Every row names the cause and the count. ## Acceptance criteria
1. A newborn row yields no death reason; a fold reads as finished; a daemon-scope death names the daemon kill; a stale lane-scope line is never the cause.
2. The park note lands once across processes; a fresh `Loops` over a parked store writes nothing.
3. A parked wall lane is resumed at its reset time; a handoff is consumed; a finished lane yields no rescue.
4. A machine-ended lane is resumed once per cause under the gate; a second death parks with the reason and its end; a lane that ended by its own work is left to the owner.
5. The nine live cards are resumed or parked with a reason and an end, in the close-out; the suite, the ratchets and `tsc` are green.

## Loop
We think resuming machine-ended lanes under the gate will change the count of Hello Revenue lanes sitting ended with nothing folded at the morning read from eight toward zero, because every one of tonight's endings was the machine's and every cause has an end the machine can read. Loop: the morning count of lanes ended with nothing folded on Hello Revenue's Planned column — session read the board's broken count and the card histories at the first morning after the fold, then daily for a week, by 2026-09-13. ## Rulings
Recorded before the build, from the conversations; each overturnable by the owner on the card.
1. **The machine resumes only a death it can name — of the latest life, on unfinished work.** Disposition is read before recovery: a recorded close is finished work whatever the lane's row says, an owner's decision outstanding is his, and a cause nothing names is unresolved and never a permission to resume. Plan 03's "never a guess" is kept; its "the owner's choice is Resume or Look" for a memory kill was written before the dial existed, and the owner asked tonight for the opposite ("I don't think they revive by themselves"). Rejected: resuming without the gate — a revived lane on a full machine is the next kill, and tonight the retry into the daemon scope made each kill take more than one lane; the owner's Resume click as the way — eight cards sat up to six hours through a wall reset nobody was awake for; unbounded retries — the horizon stays, what changes is that a park has an end.
2. **One plan for four suggestions.** All four are what one loop remembers and does; four lanes would each fix an instance and re-read the same functions. Rejected: four fix lanes, in the order the dial happened to take them.
3. **A park is a machine fact with an end, never a verdict on the owner.** "Yours (Resume)" was the honest word when nothing could lift a park; now the board knows what it waits on and says so. Rejected: keeping "yours" and adding a reminder.
4. **The standing pile is swept once at the close, by the loop's own test.** The owner, on the Discuss door, 2026-09-08: "I agree with your rec", asked whether this card would resurrect the lanes that failed before it. It would not have: nothing looks at a dead lane — `_rescue` handles MOVING lanes only, and the hour filters rescue records, not deaths — so work that died before this mechanism existed stays where it fell. So at the close the lane reads every Hello Revenue lane sitting ended with nothing landed and applies ruling 1 to each from its evidence, not from the board's old words: the transcripts of the 21 on 2026-09-08 showed 9 closed and finished (the board said "finished its turn, start again"), 1 waiting on the owner's ruling (#452), 2 walls past their reset (#409, #419), 3 whose oom-kill was not their last life (#426, #429, #435 were resumed into the daemon scope and died again there; the latest ending is what is named, or nothing is), 4 nothing on the machine names (#249, #341, #416, #417), 2 the owner stopped. A named interruption on unfinished work is resumed through the new path with the evidence on the card, or parked with a reason and an end when the machine is full; a finished card gets true words and nothing else; everything the machine cannot name, and everything that is his, goes to him once, as one batch with the evidence attached, never resumed. The close-out names every one by number, evidence, disposition and time. Rejected: leaving the pile to the Resume door — twenty-one clicks on cards whose evidence the machine already holds; resuming unknown causes in the sweep, which ruling 1 forbids and Codex's reading refused.
5. **The hour keeps its count and gets its reason.** The horizon was set in slice 03 (2026-09-04) with its count — "one automatic retry per run-out", a second death of one cause inside it parks instead of thrashing — and no written reason for the length. The hour is a chosen retry policy, not evidence that a cause has cleared: an elapsed hour is never described as an hour of successful work, and neither reading could show that two memory kills inside an hour come from one continuously full machine, so the plan does not claim it. Item 5 writes beside the number that it is policy, revisable under the loop, which tests repeated failures; a number with no reason is a method nobody can revisit.
6. **The ring.** This card repairs the classification, record and recovery of the interruptions its evidence names. Adjacent, filed as suggestions with the discussion's findings as evidence and not built here: a lane on an account that cannot sign in or is held (the machine notifies, the board says nothing true); a session that ran out of room to think or whose compaction failed (no captured payload exists to classify it — the 8 MB branch in `runtime/launch.py` is a transcript-loading limit, not a detector); a hook that arrives late and the queue's seven-day drop. Not filed: general clock-correction detection — nothing observed, only a calculation. An unrecognised ending stays unrecognised; it never becomes permission to resume.
7. **What a turn ended with is read from the records, and their absence proves nothing.** A current close (`close_landed`) is finished.

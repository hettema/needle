## Intent

The owner reads the board without opening cards: each collapsed card says whether it can start and lets him start it or plan it from there; defects and ideas are two scans, not one; the head and the column names never leave the screen; and a card's document event — archived, or a plan written for it — moves the card by the machine, with the reason on its history.

### 1. An archived document moves its card
As its suggestion says. Done means: on the corpus's archived effect, a card outside a shipped column with no live lane moves to Executed when it carries DELIVERED and a readable WATCH, otherwise to Decision moment with "its plan was archived, but no session wrote it up on the board"; an audit row names the document; a test archives an Up next card's document and finds it in Decision moment on the next read.

### 2. Defects are their own rail
As its suggestion says. Done means: `**Kind:** defect|idea` on a suggestion document (absent reads idea); a pinned defects rail at the top of Backlog with its count; the attention rail counts unplanned defects and ideas apart; old suggestions get their kind read from their text where the lane can tell, with its table of guesses in the close-out.

### 3. The collapsed card says whether it can start now
As its suggestion says. Done means: one pill on every Planned and Up next card — free, collides with #N, no gate, nowhere to run — computed by the Start door's own function; Start on a free card's collapsed face; "Start anyway" only on the open face; a gateless document's open card shows Start closed with its reason.

### 4. The head and the column names stay on screen
As its suggestion says. Done means: head, attention rail and column headings pinned; each column scrolls on its own; an open card stays in view; on a laptop the head folds to one line after the first scroll; a page test scrolls a column and asserts the heading and the rail are in the viewport.

### 5. The Plan door, and the card follows the plan
As its suggestion says. Done means: Plan on every suggestion card, collapsed and open, opening a plan-writing conversation with the brief the suggestion specifies; "Plan these together" over a selection; when a plan citing a live suggestion lands, the watcher relinks the card (same number, same history), archives the suggestion beside it with "carried by <plan>", and moves the card to Planned by the machine; a plan citing several suggestions relinks the first and marks the others folded into it, grouped under its card until it closes; the attention rail counts suggestions without a plan. **Ruling:** this slice's own six suggestions are the first to be folded this way: this plan cites them, so when it lands the watcher relinks and folds them — the lane verifies that on its own card first. **Interim state to migrate:** on 2026-09-04 the coordinating session archived the nine carried suggestions naming their plans and parked their cards (#7, #8, #10, #12, #13, #14, #15, #18, #19) in Not now with a "Folded into" RULING row, because Backlog must hold only ideas no plan holds yet; when folding lands, the lane gathers those cards under their plans' cards from that state, so Not now is not their home.

### 6. A write from another process reaches the page within a second
As its suggestion says (a defect). Done means: the server checks `PRAGMA data_version` on its own connection once a second and bumps its version when another process committed; a test writes a row through a second `Store` and sees the bump; the page's reconnect after a restart re-reads once. Every command-line door's write is on the page within a second, with no change to the writers.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Archive an Up next card's document with no lane: Decision moment on the next read, reason on the row.
2. A suggestion with `Kind: defect` sits on the defects rail; the rail counts it; the attention rail counts it apart from ideas.
3. Every Planned and Up next card shows its pill; a free card starts from its collapsed face; the gateless open card shows why Start is closed.
4. Scroll a long Backlog: the head, the rail and every column heading stay; Executing does not move.
5. Press Plan on a suggestion: a window opens with the plan-writing brief; when the plan file lands citing the suggestion, the same card is in Planned with the plan as its document and the suggestion archived; a plan citing three suggestions leaves one relinked card and two folded under it.
7. The lane's own plan, which cites six suggestions, relinks and folds them when it lands; the close-out says which card kept the number.

## Rulings
Recorded as the build makes them, each with the alternative rejected.

1. **The archived rule lives with the machine's moves, not in the corpus sweep.** `board/lane.py::after_archive` runs in the lane loop beside `exit_for`, on every read, for a card in Backlog, Planned, Up next or Executing whose link is archived and whose lane is not hands on: to Executed on `close-landed` (DELIVERED and a readable WATCH), else to Decision moment on a new predicate, `document-archived`, re-tested on every read (the document still archived, no hands on). Rejected: acting in `Live.rescan` on the archived effect — `needle close` rescans first, in a process that has never read the machine, and would have moved the card to Decision moment a moment before the close moved it to Executed. Rejected: acting from Decision moment or Not now — the one already has the owner's eye and the other is his ruling; the machine would fight him.
2. **The corpus's effects reconcile at once.** A rescan with effects calls the loops' reconcile, so a plan that lands or a document archived is moved and doored within the second, not at the 30-second floor. `on_store_change` became `on_change`.
3. **The defects rail is a group in the store, kept by the corpus.** Backlog gains one machine-made group, `Defects`, before every group the owner named (position −1); a suggestion is born there when its document says `Kind: defect`, and on every sweep a Backlog card whose group and document disagree is rehomed (a `Rehomed` effect, a corpus move with the reason). A hand move that disagrees with the line is refused at once with the file to edit, so the board never undoes his drag later in silence. Rejected: a view that filters defects out of their groups — the drop preview and the store's positions then disagree, breaking slice 01's "a dropped card lands where the preview said"; a column — width the laptop does not have (the suggestion's own ruling). The page furls the rail to its one line by default: ideas are one scan, and opening the rail is the other.
4. **A suggestion without a `Kind:` line is read from its text where the text can tell.** A `Found by` naming a review (the rings rule files defects from reviews) or a defect-shaped title (does not, cannot, no longer, misses, fails, escapes, race, blind, hole, bypass, broken, wrong, regression, crash, bug, defect) reads as a defect; everything else is an idea; a `Kind:` line always wins. The table of what this read on Hello Revenue's 149 live suggestions is in the close-out; the fix for a wrong guess is one line in the document.
5. **The pill and the Start door are one judgment.** `doors_for` names the pill's word in the same branch that opens or closes Start (`Readiness` on `Doors`: free, collides, no gate, nowhere to run, lane exists, elsewhere, unread), and the collapsed card shows it in Planned and Up next only. `collides` names the other lane's card and the files it is on (the collision now carries card numbers, not labels). Before the loop's first read the pill says "not read yet" rather than nothing. Start on the collapsed face is the same door with the same reason as its title; "Start anyway" stays on the open face; a gateless queued card's open face shows Start closed with its reason in text.
6. **The board never writes into a project's repository.** The Plan door's brief tells the session to move each carried suggestion to `done/` with a `Carried by:` line in the commit that lands the plan; the watcher relinks and folds from the plan's head alone and tolerates a carried suggestion left live (never born, never "without a card"). Rejected: the watcher renaming files in the owner's checkout and committing on his behalf — a read with a write into another repository as its side effect is the shape of 0.1's deepest trap, and an uncommitted rename in the main checkout stops the trunk sync (plan 07, ruling 1). This is the one clause of item 5 built otherwise than its suggestion wrote it.
7. **A plan carries what its head names.** `Document.cites` reads suggestion paths from the head fields only (`Written:`, `Carries:`); a plan's body names neighbouring suggestions in prose without carrying them. The plan's card is, in order: the card already linked to it, the card its `**Card:**` line names, the first cited suggestion's card; the other cited cards fold under it. A live plan carries before an archived one; a shipped card (Executed, Done) is never carried; a card already folded is not re-carried.
8. **A folded card takes no slot.** It keeps its row, number and history, its `folded_into` names the leader, and its group follows the leader's on every move by the same hand and on the same evidence ("followed #N, into which it is folded"); the layout excludes it, so a move cannot land between it and the cards the page shows, and a move of the folded card itself is refused with the leader named. A plan that later names a folded card by number unfolds it. Folded cards are skipped by the machine's moves, the signal loop, the verdicts and the attention counts; the page shows them under their leader on both faces.
9. **A plan appearing promotes a card from Backlog or Not now only.** A suggestion card the owner had queued in Up next keeps its rank when its plan lands; the plan becomes its document where it stands. Rejected: moving every relinked card to Planned — Up next is his gate.
10. **Item 6 is a write stamp, not `PRAGMA data_version` alone.** Every commit, from any process, bumps one row (`writes`: seq, origin, at) through a session hook, and the server polls it once a second, subtracting its own commits by their stamps (`Store.own_commits_upto`), so foreign commits = Δseq − Δown, exactly, whatever the interleaving. `data_version` cannot tell the server's pooled connections from another process, and the mechanism this replaces proved why that matters: the store-file watcher heard the lane loop's own lane records land, re-read, wrote again, and bumped the page's version 2.3 times a second for as long as the server ran (measured on the served board at 17:09, versions 1347→1405 in 25 s idle; the throwaway board under this build sat at 6 for 6 s and rose by exactly one on one foreign write). The file watcher is gone: one way to hear a write.
11. **A reconnected stream re-reads once.** The page forgets the version it knew when its stream reopens, so the first message after a restart always re-reads, even when the new server's counter lands on the old number. Before this the reconnect worked by coincidence of numbers.
12. **The head is pinned by giving the columns the scroll.** The page never scrolls; the head and the attention line are pinned, `.board` takes the remaining height, each column scrolls on its own with its head and note sticky inside it, and the Triage lens scrolls itself. Below the wide breakpoint the head folds to one line — wordmark, project, Idea, the attention counts with their words dropped by style and kept as titles — while any column is scrolled, and unfolds at the top. The comp (2026-09-03-the-board.html) is amended in these words, not redrawn: pinned head, per-column scroll, the defects rail's line at the top of Backlog, the pill and the two doors on the collapsed foot, the folded head. `tools/scroll_check.py` is the page test the suggestion asked for, since jsdom lays nothing out; the vitest suite holds the fold's state.
13. **A plan-writing conversation is a discussion of kind `board-plan`.** `Discussion.kind` is the window kind it was opened through (one primitive reused, migration 0006); a Plan for several cards is one row per card under one session, one line on the rail ("Plan #7, #8"). The brief names the project's own plan-writing skill when `.claude/skills/` holds a directory named with both "plan" and "write" (Hello Revenue's `hr-plan-write`), else the shape in `docs/plans/README.md`.
14. **Sequenced after 05, built after 07.** 07 landed first (its ruling 7); this lane found the Idea door, the watercooler and the conversations list in place and added Plan beside them. The parked interim cards gather under their plans on the first sweep after the fold, from Not now, by rule 7: this plan's six under #16, 07's two under #17, 08's two under #20.

## Intent

Needle is the home of the loops, and the loops actually live there. A shipped card's signal is read by the machine on its cadence wherever a reader exists, and the owner is asked only where no machine can read, as one batched question with one click each way. Every machine-placed status shows the evidence it rests on, and doubts itself the moment that evidence is gone. And the card's doors are where the owner's hand already is.

### 1. Every machine-placed status names its evidence and doubts itself

From `docs/slice-suggestions/2026-09-04-every-machine-status-names-its-evidence-and-doubts-itself.md` (archive it with this plan). Done means: the predicate behind each machine-placed column is one function in `board/` over the loop's facts; every machine move records which predicate it satisfied; on every read each machine-placed card is re-tested and a card whose evidence is gone carries a doubt mark on the page with the missing fact in words, and counts in the attention rail, independent of any move; the owner's own placements are never doubted; the 0.1 import's placements in machine columns start as "evidence unknown" until the first read confirms or doubts them; a test kills a lane's process and sees the doubt on the next read.

### 2. The doors are at the top, and a window that is open is a door too

Today the doors render below the record, and Watch disappears when a window is already open, with its reason in a tooltip nobody hovers ("A window into this session is already open" was the exact state when the owner looked for it on #387). Done means: the doors row sits directly under the card's title on the open card, before the summary; a live lane whose window is open offers **Focus its window** (the compositor brings the tab forward, through the runtime, proved by the focused window's app-id) in Watch's place; every closed door that the owner would expect on this card (Watch, Answer, Look, Resume) is shown closed with its reason in text, not hidden; the comp for the open card is amended and re-signed in the plan's Rulings, not rebuilt.

### 3. The shipped cards' loops are readable

Done means: each of the 54 prose WATCH rows on Hello Revenue's Executed cards is rewritten in the signal grammar (`<what> — url|file|command|owner <target> [expect …] by YYYY-MM-DD [every Nh]`), through `needle row`, never by editing a card by hand, with the original text kept in the card's history; the rule for the rewrite is written first and applied uniformly: a signal a machine can read (a file in `docs/plans/done/`, a URL, a command in the project's own tooling with a stated expectation) becomes that reader; everything else becomes `owner`, with the question as the `what`. **Ruling:** owner questions are batched — the attention rail says "N shipped cards wait on your reading" and opens one list with one click each way per card, never N separate asks; due dates for translated rows are today plus seven days unless the original names a real event (a build, a deploy, a date), which is kept. The close-out carries the table: card, original text, reader chosen, why.

### 4. Hello Revenue's close ritual writes the grammar

Done means: the `hr-plan-execute` skill's close step and the one line in `CLAUDE.md` that names the WATCH row teach the grammar with one example of each reader kind, so no future close writes prose again; edited with a commit on that repository's `develop` only when its tree is clean and card #387's lane has folded, otherwise the exact edit goes in this plan's close-out for the coordinating session. Needle's own `README.md` shows the same grammar in one place, and `needle close` refuses a WATCH row it cannot parse, saying the grammar.

1. Kill a lane's process by hand: the card shows a doubt within one read, with the missing fact in words, and the attention rail counts it; after the loop moves the card the doubt is gone.
2. The open card shows its doors under the title; with a window open, Watch reads "Focus its window" and clicking it brings the tab forward.
3. Re-run the measurement: 54 of 54 WATCH rows on Executed cards parse; the attention rail shows one batched owner question, not dozens.
4. `needle close` with a prose WATCH row is refused with the grammar in the message.
5. Hello Revenue's close instructions carry the grammar, or the exact edit is in the close-out.

## Rulings

Recorded as the build made them (Claude Fable 5.1 at high, 2026-09-04), each with the alternative rejected.

1. **The predicate behind each machine-placed column is one function in `board/evidence.py`, and every machine move names the predicate it satisfied on its own audit row.** The audit row gained a nullable `evidence` column (migration 0004) and the store refuses a machine move that names none; a read asks the named predicate again. Rejected: inferring the predicate from the column alone (a machine move back to Up next rests on "the lane ended", which Up next itself says nothing about); a table of doubts (a doubt is derived on every read and never stored — stored, it would outlive its own evidence, the very failure it exists to catch).

2. **The owner's placements are never tested. The 0.1 import's placements in Executing and Executed are tested by their column's predicate; its placements in Done and Decision moment are his word and are trusted.** 0.1's grammar made Done the owner's move ("verification stays yours — the board surfaces, never concludes"), and Decision moment had two makers the card file cannot tell apart. Rejected: doubting the three imported Done cards (his placements, on 0.1's word which was his).

3. **The mover and the doubt read the same facts, so a plain death is moved in the read that sees it and the doubt never shows; the doubt speaks where the mover waits or is refused.** A lane killed mid-close — DELIVERED written this life, the plan not yet archived — stays in Executing (a close still landing, plan 03) and reads "the board doubts this: no live session has hands on its worktree"; when the close lands, the loop moves the card and the doubt is gone. That is the acceptance test. Rejected: computing the doubt inside the loop before its moves (stale the moment a move lands); doubting a placement the machine made in the same read.

4. **The re-test asks for the facts that can vanish — the process, the archive, the DELIVERED row, the readable signal, the reading — and not for "this life of the lane", which the mover checked when it moved and which cannot un-happen.** Rejected: reading every card's whole history on every board read (the placement is one row per card, read in one query).

5. **A window that is open is a door: Watch's slot reads "Focus its window", opens through a new runtime verb (`needle focus`) and proves itself by the compositor's active window.** Hyprland here runs a Lua config and `hyprctl dispatch` takes Lua (verified live 2026-09-04: `hl.dsp.focus({window = w})` through `hyprctl eval`, the window looked up by address; the proof is `hyprctl activewindow -j` carrying that address, which landed within a second across a workspace switch). Rejected: a closed Watch with the reason in a tooltip (the state the owner met on #387); opening a second window (the runtime never opens two for one session).

6. **The doors row is the first thing under the title on the open card — every door, Open the plan, Copy path, Move to — and every door the owner would expect on a card with a lane (Watch, Answer, Look, Resume, Stop) and Discuss say why in text, in one line under the row, when they cannot open.** Comp 2 (`docs/design/2026-09-03-the-card.html`), call 7 — "the actions row holds only what this slice can honestly do", at the bottom of the card — is amended by this ruling: the row moves under the title and holds every door slice 03 built; the file stays frozen (`docs/design/README.md`) and this ruling is its re-signature. Rejected: rebuilding the comp; a separate reasons panel; Answer's box moved out of the lane section (the question is its context).

7. **The signals only the owner can read are one list, opened from the attention line ("N shipped cards wait on your reading"), one click each way per card; his reading goes through the same door the card offers and the board is re-read after.** Rejected: N separate asks; a third "not yet" click (a card whose event has not happened simply stays in the list, and the owner answers what he can).

8. **The rewrite rule for the 54, applied uniformly.** R1: a signal becomes a machine reader only when a reader on this machine can read the named fact now — `file` for a file's existence in the repository, `url` for a public URL's answer, `command` for a command in the project's own tooling that prints the fact, run once by hand while translating; everything else becomes `owner` with the question as the `what`. R2: an owner `what` is one question answered with one click, never carrying a word the grammar reads as a reader. R3: a calendar date in the original is kept, otherwise today plus seven days (2026-09-11); owner signals carry no cadence. R4: a row that records a settled ruling and one open criterion — the open criterion is the signal. R5: a row about 0.1's board, retired 2026-09-04 — the question is whether the behaviour held on 0.1 before the retirement. Result: 2 command readers, 52 owner questions. Rejected: forcing readers (a URL carrying the site's headline is not the phone look #231 asked for; a green scheduled run is not the delivered email of #230 when a quiet day is green too).

9. **A rewritten DELIVERED, WATCH or REVIEW row keeps its whole previous text on the history row ("— it read: …").** Rejected: the 140-character cut the history took before (the original would have been gone from the card the moment it was translated).

10. **Hello Revenue's two instruction files are amended in this close-out, not by this lane.** Card #387's lane folded during this build and already wrote the grammar line into the skill's close step; the per-reader examples are the remaining edit, and a lane's git never targets another repository (the worktree guard). The coordinating session commits it on that repository's `develop`.

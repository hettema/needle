## Intent

Auto-fix is your standing ruling about what enters execution without you, and today it has two settings — on or off, and how many at once — so "on" means every verified defect on the column, gravest first, until the column is empty. On Hello Revenue that is 30 cards that reach a client or money and then 48 that reach only you or a session; you ruled on 2026-09-15 that the second kind waits for a signal and is fixed when a session is in that code anyway, and nothing on the board can hold that ruling: the moment the top band empties the beat walks below it, and the only way to stop it is to remember to turn the switch off, which is a wish.

After this plan the auto-fix setting has a line per board, named in the words of the grade's bands — harm outside, lies, loses, costs, looks — set where the switch is set and shown beside it. The beat takes only a verified defect whose band is at or above the line; a card below it shows on its face that it is filed and waiting, never that the board stopped or that it is yours; the head's count of defects fixing themselves reads the line the same way the beat does, so the column and the beat never disagree. Every board's line starts where auto-fix reaches today — every verified defect — so nothing changes behaviour by this landing; Hello Revenue's line is drawn at harm outside from your ruling; any other board's moves only when you move it. Moving the line is a change on the same row as flipping the switch, audited and undone the same way.

What does not change: what the mark means, who verifies it, the grade and the band ladder, the order within the column, the memory floor, the number of fix lanes, and readings — a reading enters nothing and opens on every board whatever its line (#100, ruling 5).

## Rulings

1. **A line per board, its default where auto-fix reaches today; Hello Revenue's at harm outside; the line beside the switch.** The owner, 2026-09-15, at Hello Revenue's Idea door (conversation 33980f33): "I'll go with your rec for 149", on the reading's (B). Rejected: (A), a default of harm outside on every board, because under it Needle's own auto-fix, switched on, plans nothing (#66, #71, #72 and #136 all reach him, never a client) and the 2026-09-05 standing ruling that Needle fixes itself would end without him choosing that.
2. **The line is crossed by the band alone.** A defect below the line is taken when a fresh reading grades it into a band at or above the line, or when the owner moves the line — and by nothing else. A second sighting or a recurring production signature is evidence a reading regrades from; if the regrade does not lift the band, the card waits, and is fixed when a session is in that code anyway (HOW-WE-WORK §13's adjacent seam), which is what he ruled that morning for the second kind. Rejected: a lift on how often, because it would make the line a second order beside #100's and his ruling named the band. Rejected: a lift on a `when` trigger, because that is the mark's own grammar and the beat already reads it. This was the one question asked at this door; it is the session's reading of the suggestion's own "done" paragraph, which names a regrade above the line and nothing else, and he can overturn it on this card.
3. **The line is always a band; where none has been drawn it is the last rung.** A board with no line and a board whose line is at the bottom take the same defects and are the same fact, so they have one spelling — unlike #139's undeclared and declared-nothing, which release the same things and are not the same fact. There is no clearing word: to take every defect, move the line to the last rung. Rejected: NULL as "no line drawn", for the two spellings.
4. **The words are the band's, said as where auto-fix stops.** "stops at harm outside", "stops at lies", …; the last rung is "takes every defect", because the ladder's own word for it — nothing — means "graded as describing no failure" on a card and would read as "no line" on a switch. #100's ruling 2 kept the band off the face because the parts are the reason and the band is only the order; the line names a band because a line is a rung, not a reason, and the parts stay on the face under it.
5. **A plan written above the line and moved below it before Start holds.** As #80's review finding 1 settled for the switch: a plan is not execution until Start, and the line at the moment of Start is the ruling that applies. It starts by itself when the line moves back; nothing is thrown away.
6. **The line is the dial's fact, never the mark's or the reading's.** `routing_of` is untouched, the routing word stays "triaged now", no document is rewritten and no reading lands a line. The board reads the repository and never writes into it; a `now` below the line keeps its mark and its reading, which is why moving the line takes it on the next beat with nothing re-read.
